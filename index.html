About House Aurora

Earth & Luxury

House Aurora sits at the intersection of heritage quality and modern urban authenticity. Warm, grounded tones — stone, espresso, warm sand — create a sense of longevity rather than disposable fast fashion. The dynamic HA monogram blends script-like fluidity with structured typography, echoing modern streetwear iconography.

We source terrace silhouettes, vault-grade Air Force 1s, considered outerwear and luxury accessories. Every item is inspected, photographed and packed in the studio. Textured cardstock, bronze foil and raw cotton textures run through every touchpoint — from the box to the browser. This is a distinctly local, elevated retail identity.
